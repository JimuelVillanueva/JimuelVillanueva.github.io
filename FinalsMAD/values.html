Olympic Values

Friendship

Uniting athletes from around the world to build global connections and respect.

Respect

Competing with honor, sportsmanship, and admiration for opponents.

Excellence

Constantly pushing limits and striving to achieve the best performance.

Unity

Bringing nations together in peace, friendship, and global understanding.

Sustainability

Protecting the environment and promoting green practices in sport.

Inclusivity

Creating equal opportunities for all athletes regardless of background.
