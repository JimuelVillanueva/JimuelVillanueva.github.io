Olympic Journey

History

The Olympic Games began in ancient Greece in 776 BC in Olympia to honor Zeus.

They united Greek city-states during the “Olympic Truce” and lasted for over 1,000 years.

The games were banned in 393 AD, ending a historic era.

In 1896, Baron Pierre de Coubertin revived the modern Olympics in Athens.

Current Olympics

Today’s Olympics include over 200 nations and thousands of athletes.

They feature Summer and Winter Games, held every four years.

Modern Olympics emphasize equality, technology, and global unity.

Future

The future Olympics aim to be sustainable, inclusive, and tech-driven.

New sports, VR experiences, and smart venues will shape future games.

The Olympics will continue to unite the world through sport.
